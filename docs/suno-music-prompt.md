# 积木旷野：Suno 配乐提示词

推荐先生成适合长时间探索的纯音乐，在 Suno Create 中开启 Instrumental，将下方内容粘贴到风格描述中。音乐名称：**《在旷野慢慢飞》**。

## Style prompt

Instrumental soundtrack for a cozy miniature brick-built nature exploration game. Warm, airy, playful and quietly adventurous, like a tiny glider drifting above sunlit forests, sparkling rivers and autumn hills. 88 BPM, gentle 4/4 pulse, D major with warm extended chords. Felt piano, delicate marimba and wooden mallets, fingerpicked nylon-string guitar, soft pizzicato strings, airy synth pads and a rounded, understated bass. A simple memorable melody with plenty of breathing room, subtle organic percussion, soft stereo space, intimate clean production. Gradually introduce small variations and a gentle sense of discovery, then return to the opening motif for a loop-friendly ending. Keep the dynamics relaxed and leave room for wind and game sound effects. Purely instrumental: no vocals, speech or choir; no heavy drums, dramatic drops or sharp high frequencies.

## 使用说明

- 用作背景音乐时，保持音量低于主要操作提示，避免掩盖飞行音效。
- “loop-friendly ending”是生成方向；生成后仍需试听并修整首尾，才能确保无缝循环。
- 此文件只提供生成提示词，项目尚未加入 Suno 音乐文件。
